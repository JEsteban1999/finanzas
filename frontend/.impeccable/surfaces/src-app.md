Scope: whole Bolsillo PWA (Inicio, Registrar, Movimientos, Presupuestos, Más y subpáginas, login/registro). Visitor mode: Operate.
Audience/job: one Colombian user on Android, often outdoors, one hand, often dark mode; quick capture several times a day, calm monthly review on phone and PC. Needs large type and high contrast.
Constraints: Spanish (Colombia), voz cercana de tú; COP `$1.250.000`; existing labels/texts are test contracts; no bank feel, not empty-minimal.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A warm consumer app that talks like a person — the month is told in a sentence, not a hero metric — and refuses both the cold bank dashboard and the empty grey minimal app; exactly one saturated colour, mora, marks everything you can press.

OWN-WORLD: Warm maize-tinted ground (light) / warm cacao-dark ground (dark, first-class); white (or warm-dark) soft cards with 20px radius and soft offset shadows; Nunito rounded humanist sans, 18px base, tabular figures; mora (#B0124F light / #FF7AA8 dark) only on actionable elements; quiet semantic tints for income, warning, exceeded.

STORY: Open Bolsillo, read in one sentence how the month goes, tap the mora Registrar button with the thumb, say or type the expense, confirm the prefilled draft, see a friendly budget/savings note, done.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Inicio on a phone: greeting + month picker row; a large white "Tu mes" card whose body is a sentence with the figures in bold (entró / gastaste / ahorraste / tasa); below it the pending link and spending-by-category bars; the persistent bottom bar with a raised round mora Registrar button in the centre.

FORM: digital-design-canon-warm-consumer-app-surface (catalog challenger chosen by user; my list position n/a), seed key b74d47c5.

Signature interaction: soft spring press (scale 0.97, 160ms) on every tappable card/button; the Registrar draft card rises into place when the AI returns.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
